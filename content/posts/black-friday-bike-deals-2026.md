## What's actually worth buying on Black Friday

**Wear parts you'll need anyway.** Chains, cassettes, brake pads, tires and sealant are the safest Black Friday buys, because you'll use them no matter what. If you're on SRAM, my [Red vs Force E1 breakdown](/posts/sram-red-vs-force-e1-weight/) shows the Red chain and Paceline X rotors are the cheapest weight savings in the group, and they're often discounted.

**Groupsets and wheels.** These get the biggest dollar discounts of the year, especially last year's models. Check compatibility before you buy. SRAM's 13-speed XPLR and Transmission groups [need a UDH frame](/posts/udh-sram-1x13-gravel-frame-compatibility/), and wide gravel rims [make your tires wider](/posts/zipp-303-xplr-sw-vs-enve-ses-3-4-tire-width-test/) than the label.

**Complete bikes, carefully.** Big discounts on bikes are usually on outgoing models. That's fine if you know your size and the spec is what you want. ## How to tell if a Black Friday bike deal is real

A "40% off" tag doesn't mean much if the item was marked up in October. Three quick checks:

1. **Compare against the 90-day low, not the "was" price.** That's exactly what the tire tracker above does. For other parts, check a price history or two other retailers.
2. **Read the condition on outlet bikes.** "Outlet," "refurbished" and "visual imperfections" can mean scratches, paint damage or final sale.
3. **Check the model year.** Last year's groupset or bike at 40% off is often a better buy than this year's at 15% off, but make sure it's the version you think it is.

## FAQ

**When is Black Friday 2026?** Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026, and Cyber Monday is November 30. Most cycling retailers start their sales one to two weeks early.

**Is Black Friday a good time to buy a bike?** It's one of the best times of year for components, wheels and outgoing-model bikes. Discounts on current-year complete bikes are usually smaller.

**Do bike shops match Black Friday prices?** Many local shops will match a brand-direct or major-retailer sale price on the same item, especially for bikes they sell. It's always worth asking.
