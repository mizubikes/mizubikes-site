Freehub sound is one of those things nobody admits they care about until they hear it. Some riders want silence, some want the buzz, and a lot of us just want to know what we're signing up for before spending a couple thousand dollars on wheels. So I put three popular carbon road wheelsets back to back: the **Zipp 303**, the **ENVE SES 4.5** and the **Roval Rapide CLII**.

{{< youtube 8iqAwYwS764 >}}

Coast each one, listen, and decide which you could live with on every ride. Sound isn't a performance spec, but engagement design affects both the noise and how quickly the hub picks up when you start pedaling, and that's worth knowing before you buy.
