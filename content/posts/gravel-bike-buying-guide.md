Buying a gravel bike in 2026 is harder than it should be. The category covers everything from aero race bikes to drop-bar hardtails, the drivetrain standards changed in the last two years, and a "50mm" tire can measure 54mm depending on the rim. This page pulls together everything I've written on the subject, in the order I'd think about it if I were buying today.

## 1. Start with tire clearance

Tire clearance decides more about what a gravel bike can do than any other spec. It sets the terrain the bike can handle, how comfortable it is, and how much room you have when it gets muddy. Most current race frames clear 50mm or more. Older frames and some fast, road-like gravel bikes stop at 45mm.

- [Gravel Bike Tire Clearance Chart 2026](/posts/gravel-bike-tire-clearance-chart-2026/): max tire size for current frames, side by side.
- [Gravel Bike Tire Clearance: Real-World vs Spec](/posts/gravel-bike-tire-clearance/): how far past the official number a Specialized Crux actually goes.
- [Rim Width vs Tire Width](/posts/zipp-303-xplr-sw-vs-enve-ses-3-4-tire-width-test/): my measurements of how much a wide rim adds to tire width, and why that matters for clearance.

## 2. Check the dropout before you pick a drivetrain

If you want SRAM's 13-speed XPLR, the frame needs a UDH dropout, and there's no adapter for one that doesn't have it. This is the most common trap I see with used bikes and older framesets. Shimano GRX doesn't have this restriction.

- [Will SRAM 1x13 XPLR Fit Your Gravel Bike?](/posts/udh-sram-1x13-gravel-frame-compatibility/): how to tell from photos or the spec sheet.
- [UDH Gravel Bikes: Every Frame on SRAM's List](/posts/udh-compatible-gravel-bikes-2026/): the full compatibility list.

## 3. Choose your gearing

1x is now the default for gravel, but 2x still makes sense if you ride a lot of pavement or group rides. The real choice is between tight steps between gears and a really easy low gear.

## 5. Wheels

Wide internal rims are the trend, and they do help tire shape and stability, but they also make your tires measurably wider. Factor that into clearance before you buy.
