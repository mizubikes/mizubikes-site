**Quick answer:** for 2026, RockShox Flight Attendant comes stock only on XC bikes. The cheapest is the Orbea Oiz M10 AXS Flight Attendant at $7,499, followed by the Scott Spark RC World Cup at $8,499.99. Specialized, Trek, and Pivot all have Flight Attendant builds between $9,999.99 and $15,249.99. No 2026 trail, enduro, or e-MTB builds ship with it, so for longer travel you're looking at older models on the used market or the aftermarket upgrade.

Flight Attendant is RockShox's automatic suspension. Sensors in the fork and shock read terrain, pitch, pedaling, and gear, then switch between Open, Pedal, and Lock on their own. I covered how it rides and which XC bike I'd pick in [XC bikes with Flight Attendant: which one to buy](/posts/xc-bikes-with-flight-attendant/). This post is the full list.

## Trail and enduro: the upgrade route

RockShox relaunched Flight Attendant for trail and enduro in 2024, on the Pike, Lyrik, ZEB, and Super Deluxe. I couldn't find any 2026 trail, enduro, or e-MTB bike that ships with it, so for these you'll be fitting it yourself. US launch prices:

| Part | Price |
|---|---|
| Pike Ultimate Flight Attendant (140mm) | $1,499 |
| Lyrik Ultimate Flight Attendant (160mm) | $1,549 |
| ZEB Ultimate Flight Attendant (170mm) | $1,599 |
| Super Deluxe Ultimate Flight Attendant shock | $949 |

The fork works on a wide range of bikes, and 2023-and-newer Pike, Lyrik, and ZEB forks can be upgraded. The shock comes pre-tuned for specific frames only, including the Specialized Stumpjumper EVO (2021+), Specialized Enduro (2020+), Specialized Levo SL (2022+), Santa Cruz Hightower (2019+), Trek Fuel EX (2023+), Trek Slash Gen 6 (2023+), and Yeti SB140 (2020+).

For the full system you also need an AXS pedal sensor or power meter, and ideally an AXS derailleur. A fork and shock alone run about $2,500 before those, so on a lot of bikes it's cheaper to buy an XC bike that already has it. If you're upgrading an XC bike instead, the [XC Flight Attendant guide](/posts/xc-bikes-with-flight-attendant/) lists aftermarket fitment for the SID and SIDLuxe.

## FAQ

**What's the cheapest bike with Flight Attendant?** For 2026 in the US, the Orbea Oiz M10 AXS Flight Attendant at $7,499. In Europe, the Canyon Lux World Cup CFR LTD at €6,999.

**Do any trail or enduro bikes come with Flight Attendant in 2026?** Not that I could find. The 2022–2023 Trek Slash, S-Works Enduro, YT, and Canyon builds were the last ones. Today you'd add it aftermarket.

**Do any e-bikes come with Flight Attendant?** None as a stock build. The Super Deluxe Flight Attendant shock is tuned for the Specialized Levo SL (2022+), so that's the closest option.

**Does Flight Attendant work without a power meter?** Yes, with a pedal sensor instead. A power meter lets it learn your effort zones, which is why many of the 2026 builds include one.
