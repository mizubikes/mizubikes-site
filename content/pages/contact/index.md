The best way to reach me is by email: **[mizubikes@gmail.com](mailto:mizubikes@gmail.com)**

I read everything, though it can take me a few days to reply. Email works well for:

- **Corrections.** If a spec, price or compatibility detail in a post is wrong, please tell me and I'll fix it.
- **Build and gear questions.** I can't promise a full answer to every one, but good questions often become posts.
- **Brands and partnerships.** I'm open to reviewing products that fit what I ride and write about. I don't accept paid placements in reviews, and anything provided by a brand is disclosed in the post.

You can also leave a comment on any video on the [@mizubikes YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@mizubikes).
