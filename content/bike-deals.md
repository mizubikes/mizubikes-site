Every morning this page scans the full catalogs of bike brands' own stores and a handful of US bike shops, and pulls out what's actually marked down: complete bikes, framesets, wheels, drivetrain parts and gear. Only new, in-stock items make the list.

**How to read it:** the price is today's price for the size or color shown, and the crossed-out number is that store's own list price. **Check Amazon** looks up the same item on Amazon, where the price may differ. **New today** means the deal wasn't there yesterday. **Lower again** means the price dropped below the lowest we'd seen for that deal.
