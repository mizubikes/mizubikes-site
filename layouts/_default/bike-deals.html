How this works

  • A script reads each store's public product catalog once a day and keeps in-stock items priced at least {{ $d.min_discount_pct | default 20 }}% under that store's list price. Anything under $20, and anything claiming more than 80% off, is left out. New gear only: used, demo and pre-owned items aren't listed, and neither are old standards (tubular, rim brake, 10-speed and below, 26-inch) or model years more than two years back.
  • Only established brands are listed: the ones covered in articles on this site, plus the major names in bikes, parts and gear.
  • Recently ended lists deals that were on this page in the last week and have since dropped out of the scan, either because they sold out or the price went back up.
  • New today means the deal wasn't listed yesterday. Lower again means the price dropped below the lowest we'd seen for that deal.
  • The crossed-out price is the store's own list price, not a price we verified elsewhere. Some shops set list prices high, so compare before you buy on big-ticket items.
  • The lists above show the most relevant deals. The search box covers every deal the scan found, including older standards and model years that are left off the main lists.
  • Deals aren't ranked by discount alone. Current gear from brands and models this site covers ranks higher, and so do items with several sizes still in stock. Small spares, single front or rear parts and discounts over 60% rank lower. There's a cap per store so one shop can't fill the list. Prices can change during the day and don't include shipping or tax.
  • Every button goes straight to the store that has the deal. Some store links may be affiliate links, which support the site at no extra cost to you and never change which deals are listed or how they're ranked.
