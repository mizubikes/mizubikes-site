How this works

  • The tires here are the ones winning big gravel races in 2026 and topping the major tire tests, limited to tires with a US store price that can be checked every morning. Prices are per tire, not per pair.
  • 90-day range is the lowest and highest price seen in the last 90 days. Deal means the tire is at least 10% under its typical price, or marked down 15% or more from list. 90-day low needs two weeks of history before it shows.
  • Amazon prices aren't listed because they change through the day — tap the Amazon button to see yours. Amazon links are affiliate links; buying through them supports the site at no extra cost to you.
  • Reference prices come from Worldwide Cyclery's store, and from inCycle, a Southern California Specialized dealer, for Specialized tires. Shipping and tax aren't included.
  • Race and test notes come from Schwalbe's Unbound 2026 results, Rouleur, Silca, Velo, BikeRadar, Cyclingnews, and road.cc.
